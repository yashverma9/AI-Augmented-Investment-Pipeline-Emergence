# Macrobite
The fastest way to actually get your macros right

**Call: Watch**


## What they do
Macrobite is the fastest way to get your macros right. Snap a photo of your meal and get an instant breakdown of calories, protein, carbs, and fat. Not perfect on the first try? Quick editing closes the gap in seconds, no scrolling through massive food databases. Prefer to talk? Log meals by voice, including through Siri. Track your day from your iPhone widget or Apple Watch without opening the app. Built for people who want accurate macros without the friction of traditional tracking apps.
