## What they do
The NS1 Assessment is a personalized assessment tool that will give you a breakdown of how your system responds to stress, what this means for your specific situation & what you can do to improve. In 5 minutes you'll receive: - Your regulation score: a realistic baseline of how well your system settles after stress - A five-skill scorecard: your strengths and growth edges across the core skills - A personalized path: a structured learning journey to increase your capacity and reclaim agency
