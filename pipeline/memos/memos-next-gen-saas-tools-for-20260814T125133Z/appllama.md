## What they do
Appllama is a design-research platform for app creators. Search 30,000+ screens from 700+ of the App Store’s top-earning iOS apps; follow complete onboarding, paywall, home and in-app flows; inspect UI elements, colors and fonts; and see revenue, downloads and ratings in context. New apps arrive weekly, captured live from the store. Start free no card required.
