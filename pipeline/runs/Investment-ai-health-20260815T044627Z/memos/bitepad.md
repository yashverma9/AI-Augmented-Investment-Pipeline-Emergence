# BitePad
Minimalist voice calorie tracker

**Call: Watch**


## What they do
BitePad is intentionally minimal. Instead of packing in bloated features, we focus on one thing: making calorie tracking fast and effortless. Simply speak or type what you ate, and BitePad turns it into a meal log in seconds. Unlike AI photo logging, which often misidentifies foods and requires manual corrections, BitePad lets you review the estimated calories and macros, make any quick edits if needed, and save your meal. Simple by design, built for everyday use.
