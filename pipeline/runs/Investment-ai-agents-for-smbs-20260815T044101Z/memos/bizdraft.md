## What they do
BizDraft is an AI contract assistant for small business owners and freelancers. No generic templates to fine-tune with a lawyer — every document is tailored to your specific business, state laws included, in about 90 seconds. Draft agreements, get a plain-English read on contracts you receive, adjust terms, and catch risks before signing. It's entirely yours: free, fully editable, in whatever format you need.
