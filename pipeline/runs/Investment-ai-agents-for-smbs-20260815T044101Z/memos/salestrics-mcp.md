## What they do
Without live CRM, inbox, and financial context, AI agents operate completely blind. Salestrics MCP (Model Context Protocol) connects tools like Cursor, Claude Desktop, and VS Code directly to your live workspace. Empower your agents to query pipeline data, update deal stages, trigger automations, and sync across tools like Stripe and Slack—all through one secure, org-scoped connection. Stop tab-switching and copying context. Connect your agents today at salestrics.com.
