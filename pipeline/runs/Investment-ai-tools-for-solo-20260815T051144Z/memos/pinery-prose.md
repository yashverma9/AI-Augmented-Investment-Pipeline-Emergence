# Pinery Prose
AI co-author for books + every edit is a diff you approve

**Call: Take a meeting**


## What they do
Pinery is the self-publishing studio for Mac. 2.0 adds Prose, an AI co-author that works from the pages you've already written — every edit arrives as a diff you accept or reject. Write, design, and export ePub 3 or PDF. Your files stay plain Markdown.
