## What they do
Zinng is a 24/7 AI receptionist for small businesses. It answers every call, books appointments, captures leads, filters spam, and hands off to a human when it matters. Outbound web calls, two-way SMS, website chat widget, recordings, and transcripts all live in one inbox. iOS and Android apps coming soon. Born in our own dental office, not a Silicon Valley accelerator. Fully self-funded and built for trades, home services, dental, medical, legal, and more. Live in minutes from $49/mo.
